# Grid Fundamentals

What the grid is, and the geometric commitments it rests on.

ITACaRT begins from a refusal. Most discrete global grid systems start by
wrapping a polyhedron around a sphere and subdividing its faces; ITACaRT
tessellates the WGS84 ellipsoid directly, with no intermediate solid and no
spherical approximation. The standards call this direct surface tessellation,
and it is the decision every other property in this section follows from — the
equal areas, the parallelogram cells, the decimal ladder, and the places where
the guarantees stop.
